Common questions

How do I save my sessions to Apple Health?

Allow Inhale to write Mindful Minutes when it asks, and keep Save to Apple Health switched on in Inhale's Settings. Sessions longer than a minute are saved; shorter ones still appear in Inhale's own History.

Why don't I see my heart rate?

The before-and-after heart rate comes from readings your Apple Watch takes on its own — there is nothing to install on the Watch. If it never appears, open Health → Sharing → Apps → Inhale and switch on Heart Rate, then wear your Watch while you practise. At rest the Watch only samples every few minutes, so a very short session may have no reading near it.

Where are my presets and routines?

A preset appears at the top of the mood it belongs to — a saved box-breathing pattern, for example, sits under Calm and focused. Routines you build live under Routines, and all of them can be renamed, reordered or deleted in Settings.

What is the BOLT test?

A simple measure of how comfortable your body is with carbon dioxide: after a normal breath out, how long until you feel the first clear urge to breathe. You'll find it at the top of Performance. Test at the same time each day, ideally just after waking, and your History tab charts the trend.

Does Inhale collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your iPhone and in Apple Health. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking — the full details are in the privacy policy.

Safety

Inhale is a training tool, not a medical device. Never practise breath holds or power breathing in or near water, while driving, or while standing. Stop if you feel dizzy or unwell, and talk to a doctor before starting if you are pregnant or have a heart, lung or blood-pressure condition.
