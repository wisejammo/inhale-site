Privacy Policy

Forge — strength training program and coach for iPhone
Last updated 29 September 2026

Forge collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app contains no networking code and makes no requests to any server — mine or anyone else's. Everything it records stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

Forge saves the following on your device only:

  • Your workouts — date, session, duration, and how the session felt
  • The sets you log — exercise, weight, reps, reps in reserve, and rest taken
  • Your program settings — start date, days per week, program length, equipment, units, and exercise swaps
  • Your preferences, such as coach voice and style, haptics, and rest-timer choices

This data lives in the app's own storage on your iPhone. It is covered by your device's encryption, and it is included in an iCloud or local device backup if you have backups switched on. I never receive it and have no way to see it.

Apple Health

If you grant permission, Forge uses Apple Health in two ways:

  • Writing: each finished session is saved as a Traditional Strength Training workout, with an estimate of the active calories it burned.
  • Reading: your most recent body weight, used only to make that calorie estimate.

Forge does not read heart rate or any other Health data. Health data is handled entirely by Apple's HealthKit on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere, never shared with any third party, and never used for advertising or marketing. You can grant or revoke each permission at any time in Health → Sharing → Apps → Forge, or turn saving off in Forge's Settings, and the app keeps working either way.

Coach, notifications and Lock Screen

The voice coach is generated on your iPhone by Apple's built-in speech synthesis; nothing you do is recorded and the app never uses the microphone. The "Rest's up" alert is a local notification scheduled on your device, and the Lock Screen rest timer is a Live Activity updated by the app itself. No push service or server is involved.

What is not collected

  • No name, email address, or account — there is nothing to sign up for
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage telemetry
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind

Beta testing

If you test Forge through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with me, under Apple's privacy policy. That is the only information I receive, and only when you send it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. Workouts already written to Apple Health belong to Health rather than to Forge, and are managed there — open the Health app, find Workouts, and delete whichever entries you want.

Children

Forge is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Training and your safety

Forge is a training tool, not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical advice. Lift with good form, use a spotter or safety bars for heavy barbell work, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or chest discomfort. Speak to a doctor before starting a new training program if you have an injury, a heart or blood-pressure condition, or are pregnant.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a change in what happens to your data.
