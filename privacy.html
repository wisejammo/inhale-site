Privacy Policy

Inhale — breathwork and breath-hold training for iPhone
Last updated 19 September 2026

Inhale collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app contains no networking code and makes no requests to any server — mine or anyone else's. Everything it records stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

Inhale saves the following on your device only:

  • Your breathing sessions — technique, duration, cycles completed, and any breath-hold times
  • Your BOLT test scores
  • Presets and routines you create
  • Your settings, such as whether sounds and haptics are on

This data lives in the app's own storage on your iPhone. It is covered by your device's encryption, and it is included in an iCloud or local device backup if you have backups switched on. I never receive it and have no way to see it.

Apple Health

If you grant permission, Inhale uses Apple Health in two ways:

  • Writing: a breathing session longer than one minute is saved as Mindful Minutes.
  • Reading: heart rate samples recorded around a session — by your Apple Watch, if you wear one — so the app can show your heart rate before and after. Mindful Minutes may also be read to show your history.

Health data is handled entirely by Apple's HealthKit on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere, never shared with any third party, and never used for advertising or marketing. You can grant or revoke each permission at any time in Health → Sharing → Apps → Inhale, and Inhale continues to work with Health switched off entirely — you simply lose the Mindful Minutes logging and the heart rate readout.

Tips

Inhale is free. If you choose to leave a tip, the purchase is made through the App Store and handled entirely by Apple. I never see your name, your Apple ID or your payment details — Apple reports sales to me only as totals. A tip unlocks nothing and changes nothing in the app; it is simply a thank-you.

What is not collected

  • No name, email address, or account — there is nothing to sign up for
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage telemetry
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. Anything already written to Apple Health belongs to Health rather than to Inhale, and is managed there — open the Health app, find Mindful Minutes, and delete whichever entries you want.

Children

Inhale is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Breathing exercises and your safety

Inhale is a training tool, not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical advice. Never practise breath holds or power breathing in or near water, while driving, or while standing. Stop if you feel dizzy or unwell, and speak to a doctor before starting any breathing practice if you are pregnant or have a heart, lung, or blood-pressure condition.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a change in what happens to your data.
